Our process

How a remodel actually goes

No shortcuts and no guesswork. Every kitchen and bathroom follows the same proven sequence — so the work holds up for years, not months.

Phase 01

Design & estimate

We plan the layout, help you choose materials, and lock the scope and price before anything starts.

Phase 02

Protect & demo

We cover the rest of your home, remove the old kitchen or bath, and haul away every bit of debris.

Phase 03

Rough-in

Plumbing, electrical, framing and any structural work — done right behind the walls before we close them up.

Phase 04

Waterproof & tile

Cement board, full waterproofing, then tile. This is the step that keeps a bathroom from ever leaking — and where cheaper crews cut corners. We don't.

Phase 05

Install

Cabinets, countertops, fixtures, appliances and lighting go in — measured, level and built to last.

Phase 06

Finish & walk-through

Trim, paint, caulk and a full cleanup — then we walk the finished job with you before we call it done.
